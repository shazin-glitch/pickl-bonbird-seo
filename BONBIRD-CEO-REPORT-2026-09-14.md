## What happened to these pages — plainly

- **Pakistan pages are the proof the strategy works.** Every Lahore page we built is indexed and ranking page-one; the venue pages alone pulled ~86 clicks in 90 days at positions 4–5. This is exactly what a local-SEO page network should do.
- **Oman & Qatar pages are done right but young.** All are live, all are indexable (`index, follow`), and all sit in the sitemap so Google can find them — they simply haven't accumulated search impressions yet. Qatar's West Walk is already ranking **#3.7** on its first handful of impressions, which is a good early signal. These need *time* plus a nudge (indexing requests + Google Business Profile links + internal links from the market home/menu), not rework.
- **One genuine bug — Oman Chicken Tenders is set to `noindex`.** The page is live with full, correct content, but it tells Google not to index it — so it can never rank and it's excluded from the sitemap. This is a one-click fix in Yoast (flip to `index`), then request indexing. **Recommend fixing this now.**
- **The market totals are mostly the revamp pages, not ours (yet).** Oman's ~315 and Qatar's ~228 clicks over 90 days come overwhelmingly from the rebuilt home/menu pages. Our pages will add to that as they mature — Pakistan shows the pattern.
