FAQs

Is Bonbird District One dine-in or takeout?

Takeout and delivery only. No seating at this location. Order for pickup or get it delivered to you around District One.

What fried chicken options are on the menu?

Bone-in pieces by the bucket or box, tenders, sandwiches, wraps, and rice bowls. Every order is made fresh to order, never frozen.

How spicy can you go?

Four spice levels: Plain Jane, Medium, Hot, and XXX. If heat isn't your thing, go with a flavour instead: Garlic Parm, Jamaican Tang, Jalapeño, Lemon Pepper, or Chicken Salt.

Is the chicken fresh or frozen?

Always fresh. Always made to order. Hormone-free and antibiotic-free. No frozen stock, no shortcuts.

What sides can I add to my order?

Chicken Salt Fries, Spicy Fries, Mac and Three Cheese, Mash Potato, Bon Gravy, Slaw, Dill Pickles, Chilli Pickles, and Turmeric Rice. Add a shake to round it out.
