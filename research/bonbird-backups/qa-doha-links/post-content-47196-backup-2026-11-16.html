FAQs

Is the chicken at Bonbird West Walk fresh or frozen?

Always fresh. Never frozen. Every order is made to order using 100% hormone-free, antibiotic-free chicken. That's the standard at every Bonbird, including West Walk.

What spice levels and flavours can I choose from?

Plain Jane, Medium, Hot and XXX. Or go flavour instead: Garlic Parm, Jamaican Tang, Jalapeño, Lemon Pepper or Chicken Salt. Pick one (or two if you're grabbing a bucket), applies to bone-in, tenders, sandwiches and wraps.

Can I get Bonbird West Walk delivered to me?

Yes. Bonbird at West Walk is available for delivery around the area. Order now.

What sides are available?

Chicken Salt Fries, Bon Gravy, Mac and Three Cheese, Mash Potato, Slaw, Plain Fries, Spicy Fries, Dill Pickles and Chilli Pickles. Order a few. The Bon Gravy is not optional.

Does Bonbird West Walk do group orders or buckets?

Yes. Bone-in pieces come in 2, 3, 5, 8 or 12 piece. Tenders run the same sizes. Order a bucket, grab a table at West Walk and work it out from there.
