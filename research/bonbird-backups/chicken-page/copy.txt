What is Nashville hot chicken?
Nashville hot chicken is fried chicken coated in spicy oil straight out of the fryer. The oil soaks into the crust, so the heat lives in every bite, not just on the surface.
It goes back to 1930s Nashville and a man called Thornton Prince. As the story goes, a girlfriend he'd wronged loaded his fried chicken with extra pepper as payback. He loved it, opened a restaurant around it, and started one of the most copied fried chicken styles in the world.

Is Nashville hot chicken really that hot?
It's meant to be. XXX Hot is the top of our heat scale. Not sure? Start with Medium Spice and work your way up.
What's the difference between Nashville hot chicken and regular fried chicken?
The spicy oil. Regular fried chicken gets its flavour from the breading. Nashville hot chicken is coated in spicy oil after frying, which pushes the heat into the crust.
