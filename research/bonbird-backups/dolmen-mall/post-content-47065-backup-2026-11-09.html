FAQs

Is Bonbird chicken fresh or frozen?

Always fresh, never frozen. Every piece of chicken at Bonbird is hormone-free, antibiotic-free and cooked to order. That's the standard, every single time.

What spice levels does Bonbird DHA offer?

Four spice levels: Plain Jane, Medium, Hot and XXX. If you'd rather go by flavour, choose from Garlic Parm, Jamaican Tang, Jalapeño, Lemon Pepper or Chicken Salt. Both systems, one order.

What can I order besides chicken burgers?

A lot. Bone-in pieces by the bucket (2, 3, 5, 8 or 12 piece), tenders, wraps including the Bon Wrap and Snack-A-Wrap, rice bowls with turmeric rice, and sides like Chicken Salt Fries, Mac and Three Cheese and Bon Gravy. There's a full menu here, not just burgers.

Does Bonbird have other locations in Lahore?

Yes. Bonbird trades at Cue Cinemas, Gulberg, Dolmen Mall and Johar Town as well. Full location details are on the Lahore locations page.

What makes Bonbird different from other fried chicken spots in DHA?

Fresh chicken made to order is the foundation. Add a spice system that actually gives you control, a menu with real range from rice bowls to bone-in buckets, and sides that are worth ordering. All bird. No bull.
